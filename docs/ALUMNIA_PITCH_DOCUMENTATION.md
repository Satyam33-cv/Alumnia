## ✨ Key Features (In Plain English)

### 1. 🎯 Smart Matchmaking (Like Netflix for Careers)
Students don't need to guess who to talk to. Our smart algorithm analyzes a student's skills, interests, and goals, and instantly pairs them with the Top 5 alumni who are the best match to help them.

### 2. 🤝 The "Ask for a Referral" Engine
No more awkward LinkedIn messages. When an alumni posts a job, a student clicks "Ask Referral," attaches their resume, and writes a quick note. The alumni gets a neat request in their inbox where they can click "Accept," "Reject," or "Referred." Both sides can track the status every step of the way, right up until the student gets hired.

### 3. 🧠 Mentorship Hub
Alumni can flip a switch on their profile to say "Open to Mentoring." Students can then book time on their calendar for career advice or interview prep. 

### 4. 💬 Built-in Messaging
Once a connection is made, students and alumni can chat directly on the platform. No need to switch to WhatsApp or email.

### 5. 🏆 Success Stories Wall
A place to celebrate wins. Alumni can post about their career journey (like landing a job at Google). Faculty approve these stories, and they are displayed to inspire the entire student body.

### 6. 📅 Event & Reunion Ticketing
Universities can create events, webinars, or reunions. Alumni and students can RSVP with a single click, and organizers can track attendance limits in real-time.

### 7. 🎁 Giving & Donations
Alumni who want to give back financially can easily donate to specific college campaigns (like a "Library Renovation" or "Student Emergency Fund") through a transparent tracker.

### 8. 🛡️ The Admin Command Center
A powerful dashboard for the university. Admins can verify that users are actually real alumni, upload spreadsheets of new graduates, and see analytics on how many students were hired through the platform.
